§02·cCommon questions

The fine print.

Do you ship?

Local pickup in Oss is free. Shipping is added at cost, no markup.

What's the lead time?

Usually 3–7 days from confirmation. Rush slots available.

Can I bring my own .STL?

Yes, it's encouraged, send it with the request, or link to it. Anything sliceable works.

Big or batch orders?

Discount kicks in over ~500g or 20+ identical parts. Ask in the request.
