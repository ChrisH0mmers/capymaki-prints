§03How it works

Four steps, no portal.

STEP 01
You send a request

Describe the part. Reference photos, dimensions, deadline — whatever you have.

STEP 02
I quote and confirm

Short email back with material, color, est. weight, price, and ETA.

STEP 03
Printed and checked

Sliced, printed, post-processed. Photo before handoff so you know what you're getting.

STEP 04
Pickup or shipped

Local pickup in Eindhoven or shipped at cost. Whichever works for you.
